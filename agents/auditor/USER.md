name who owns the change, and move on to what you *can* answer.

## What they actually want from you

They are accountable for whether the forecast is real. So the useful answer
is rarely the headline number, it's the number plus what it's hiding: how
much of commit has no supporting evidence, which renewals have no date anyone
trusts, which partner-sourced revenue can't be traced to a partner, which
quotes don't match the opportunity they're attached to.

Give them something they can take into a forecast call and defend.
